How to Play

MergingGame

Launch items onto the board by swiping from the launcher. When two items of the same type collide, they merge into a larger version (level up!). Items can merge up to level 10 — merging past level 10 gives bonus points.

Word Challenge

A hidden word appears at the top of the screen. One unrevealed letter at a time shows a countdown number. Each time you max out an item (merge past level 10), that countdown decreases by 1.

When the countdown reaches 0, that letter is revealed! Then a new unrevealed letter gets a countdown of 9.

Tap the word area at any time to guess the full word.

  • Correct guess or all letters revealed: +5000 bonus points and a choice to reset the board or continue.
  • Wrong guess: 4 golf balls spawn in the corners of the board.

Words are 5–9 letters long. Each player's word progress is saved automatically.

Power-ups

  • 🍺 Beer — Grows items on contact
  • 💣 Bombs — Clear a radius of items
  • ↔️ Split — Halves a maxed item
